Cutter-sweep study

Provisional preview, not a CNC job. This raster study depicts candidate cutter sweeps, not a validated machining plan. Do not cut from it or approve it as machine-ready.

Light wood = uncut; brown = up to 0.5 mm engraving; dark = deeper removal, including the hole and cutout. Blue circles mark the four holding-tab locations. The view is a 130 mm square, not the entire stock.

  • 100 mm body, rounded hanging tab and 4 mm hole; 152.4 × 203.2 × 3.25 mm plywood.
  • Sequence: flat-bit engraving, V-bit detail, then flat-bit hole and cutout. Use separate tool setups with a common XY origin and re-zero Z after each change.
  • Black artwork is the engraving target. Flat 3.175 mm bit, then 30° V-bit (0.1 mm tip), at up to 0.5 mm depth. V-bit width at 0.5 mm: 0.368 mm; the candidate V-bit strokes use shallower depths where the raster feature is too narrow.
  • Flat-bit hole and outside profile: 3.35 mm depth, including 0.1 mm breakthrough; four 4 mm holding tabs leave 1 mm stock thickness.
  • 0.1 mm pixel resolution; conservative raster scanline centers at 0.7 mm for the flat bit and 0.1 mm for the V-bit. Sampled center-point sweeps estimate that 82.6% of the raster engraving target reaches within 0.01 mm of full depth; 94.6% sees any cut. This display does not show the exact continuous sweep. Tiny retained gaps and partial-depth V-bit edges require review. The study contains 11,000 separate original V-bit strokes, including 181 single-point strokes, joined into 7,686 routes spanning 13.4 m before retractions or additional passes; 181 are isolated points. The flat-bit candidate joins 795 original strokes (644 single-point strokes) into 293 connected routes spanning 1.06 m, including 203 isolated points. Raster-cell geometry bounds these joins, but entry, retracts and passes remain unplanned; isolated cuts are not production-ready plunges. Continuous engraving clearance is bounded against the raster target; the raster is not an exact vector or physical safety check. Entry, retraction, tool changes and the full pass-by-pass motion are not planned.

Pass schedule for discussion only

Not verified or approved. This is a proposed pass structure for review, not a CNC program or a recommendation to start the machine. Feeds, stepdowns, safe Z, clamp and holder clearances, stock placement and the machine's measured envelope still need operator confirmation.

  • Flat engraving: one 0.5 mm pass. Flat-bit hole and profile: depth caps 0.75, 1.5, 2.25, 3, 3.35 mm, with the four holding tabs capped at 2.25 mm.
  • V-bit engraving: depth caps 0.2, 0.4, 0.5 mm; shallower features retain their individual target depth. This comparison repeats only routes with remaining depth on later passes.
  • Historical ornament prototype reference: 300 mm/min cut and 100 mm/min plunge for the flat bit; a community 3018 softwood/MDF table lists 50 mm/min cut, 30 mm/min plunge and 0.2 mm depth for a 0.1 mm V-bit tip at a different, unverified spindle setting. Neither reference is validated for this plywood or the operator-reported 1,000 RPM spindle.
  • At 50 mm/min the proposed V-bit passes alone would require at least 13.2 hours of cutting movement. Retractions, plunges, acceleration, tool changes and the flat-bit work would add time.
  • Work XY zero was reported at the lower-left stock corner, with the 8-inch side along X; Z0 was set at the stock top with the V-bit installed. Preserve XY and re-establish Z after each tool change. Position within the stock is not yet fixed.

For comparison only, doubling V-bit row spacing to 0.2 mm reduces the candidate cutting length to 6.5 m, but sampled near-full-depth coverage falls to 69.1% and any-depth coverage to 93.3%. This alternative is not shown in the image or selected for machining.
